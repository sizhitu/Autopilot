US stock watchlist checklist (research only)

A watchlist is only useful if you can scan it in minutes. This checklist explains how to read a multi-column board—timing labels, trend filters, and actions—without treating any single label as an order ticket.

Disclaimer: Autopilot is a research tool. Nothing here is investment advice. You are responsible for your decisions.

What to scan first

  1. Trend filter — environment first.
  2. Timing label — whether a setup is even worth a deeper look.
  3. Price change — what already moved today / this week.
  4. Open the chart — confirm structure; do not invent a story from one cell.

Volume-price in one minute

If price range compresses while volume fades, you may be in a consolidation regime—not a guaranteed breakout direction. Treat convergence and divergence as checklist items.

US and other markets on one board

You can track US names alongside other listings, but session times and risk rules differ. Same screen ≠ same trade plan.
